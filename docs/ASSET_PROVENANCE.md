## Planned contents

The payload is deliberately tiny, because the demo must be reliable on a testnet and within
free-tier limits. It is one of:

- a small text/JSON asset (preferred — smallest, easiest to verify, easiest to diff), or
- a small image, or
- a short PDF.

**Exact contents are not yet fixed.** They will be recorded here with a source and a digest
when BUILD 2 selects them. Until then this section stays open rather than being filled in
with a guess.

---

## Provenance rules

1. **No third-party copyrighted material.** Nothing is taken from a real brand, a stock
   library, or the open web, unless its licence explicitly permits use and the licence is
   recorded here.
2. **Generated content is labelled.** If any image is produced with a generative tool, that
   is recorded, along with the tool and date.
3. **No embedded text in images.** Per the visual direction, imagery carries no text — which
   also avoids accidental trademark use.
4. **Every asset records a digest.** So a judge can confirm the recovered plaintext is
   exactly the asset that was protected.
5. **Nothing is presented as a real licence.** The asset is a prop for demonstrating a
   mechanism. It is never described as being under a real legal licence.
